10 Script Writing Tips for Viral UGC Videos

Emily Patel

@emilypatel
January 10, 2024•7 min read
# 10 Script Writing Tips for Viral UGC Videos Great scripts are the foundation of successful UGC videos. Here are 10 proven tips to make your scripts convert. ## 1. Hook Within 3 Seconds Your opening line must grab attention immediately. Try: - Asking a provocative question - Making a bold statement - Addressing a specific pain point ## 2. Speak Directly to Your Audience Use "you" language. Make it personal and relatable. ## 3. Keep It Conversational Write how people actually talk, not how they write. Include: - Contractions (don't, can't, won't) - Simple language - Short sentences ## 4. Focus on Benefits, Not Features Instead of "Our app has AI-powered analytics," say "See exactly which videos your audience loves." ## 5. Create Urgency Give viewers a reason to act now, not later. ## 6. Tell a Story Even in 30 seconds, structure your script with: - Setup (problem) - Conflict (why it matters) - Resolution (your solution) ## 7. Use Social Proof Reference numbers, testimonials, or user stats. ## 8. Include a Clear CTA Tell viewers exactly what to do next. ## 9. Optimize for Sound-Off Viewing 60% of social videos are watched without sound. Ensure your script works with captions alone. ## 10. Test and Iterate Your first script won't be perfect. Test variations and learn from the data.
